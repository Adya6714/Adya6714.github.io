Welcome to the Grimoire — a personal reading library for the ideas behind Lumenwald.

This is not a textbook. It is a guided path through the questions that structure the research program: what benchmarks measure, what they miss, and how to look inside models when scores stop being informative.

## How to use this library

Each chapter is short on purpose. Read one sitting at a time. Progress is saved automatically in your browser.

## The through-line

Across papers and projects, the same tension returns: **fluency is not understanding**, and **accuracy is not strategy**. The constellation on this site maps the projects; these chapters narrate the argument that binds them.
