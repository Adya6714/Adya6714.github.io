Leaderboards are coordination devices. They are also compression algorithms — and compression always discards structure.

## When scores agree and systems don't

Two models can share an accuracy band while disagreeing on almost every interesting intermediate decision. If you only ship the scalar, you have no language for that disagreement.

## Building a probe

Start from a failure you care about. Encode it as a minimal contrast. Require the model to expose an intermediate claim you can check. Score the claim separately from the final answer.

That separation is the whole method.

## Connecting to interpretability

Behavioral probes tell you *that* strategies diverge. Mechanistic work asks *where*. The research program treats these as sequential, not competing, lenses.

When you finish this chapter, open the constellation and inspect the edges between Same Score, Reasoning Probes, and Mechanistic Interpretability — those links are the agenda in graph form.
