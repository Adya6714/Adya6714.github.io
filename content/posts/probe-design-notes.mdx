This note documents the design constraints behind the three-probe framework: faithfulness, consistency, and compositional generalization.

## Design principles

A probe should be:

- **Discriminative** — separates systems that look identical on standard suites
- **Cheap to run** — so it can sit in continuous eval, not a one-off paper figure
- **Interpretable** — failures should point at a mechanism, not a mysterious score

```ts
type ProbeResult = {
  modelId: string;
  faithfulness: number;
  consistency: number;
  composition: number;
};
```

## Faithfulness first

Faithfulness asks whether stated reasoning matches the computation that produced the answer. We treat mismatches as first-class signals, not annotation noise.

## Consistency under paraphrase

Minor rewrites should not flip strategy class. When they do, we log the pair and the divergence mode.

## Compositional generalization

Held-out compositions of training-like subskills expose whether the model is stitching procedures or recalling surface templates.
